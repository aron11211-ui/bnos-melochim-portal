# Parent Portal Guide

## Sign in

Use the invitation or password reset link provided by the school office. After setting a password, sign in with your email and password.

## Dashboard

The dashboard shows registration progress, missing items, agreements, tuition balance, upcoming payment information, and recent school messages. Use the action buttons to go directly to the page that needs attention.

## Registration

Open **Registration** and use **Save and Continue** after each section. Progress is saved to the school system so you can return later.

## Documents

The Documents page shows required documents and their current status. Full secure upload/download workflow is still being completed.

## Agreements

Use Agreements to review school policies and acknowledgments. Full signed-copy generation is still being completed.

## Tuition

Tuition shows charges, discounts, payments, and balances that are available in the portal. Online payment processing is not connected yet.

## Help

If your family record or children are missing, contact the school office so your account can be linked to the correct family.
