# Staff Guide

## Registration Office

Use the Registration Office portal to create families, add students, review registration progress, invite parent users, and monitor missing documents. Document upload/review persistence is still being completed.

## Tuition Office

Use the Tuition Office portal to view tuition accounts and payment status. Full ledger, receipts, reversals, and payment-plan workflows are still being completed.

## School Management

School Management is intended for read-only oversight. Do not use it for operational edits.

## System Administration

System Administration can invite users and manage account role/status. Use the Users & Access page to disable, reactivate, or change roles.

## Important security reminders

- Do not share setup links publicly.
- Do not put service-role keys in frontend files, Render static variables, or GitHub.
- Use role-specific accounts when testing permissions.
